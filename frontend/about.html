ABOUT THE PROJECT

From prediction
to preparation.

CanteenIQ is an end-to-end machine learning project built to explore how historical demand and everyday conditions can support more informed food preparation in a college canteen.

We combine data, a trained regression model and a simple interface to turn inputs like calendar, weather, special days and recent demand into actionable demand forecasts.
